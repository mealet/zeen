# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone participating in the Zeen project, regardless of their background, experience, or level of expertise.

We believe that open-source development works best when people can freely exchange ideas, ask questions, discuss disagreements, and contribute without fear of harassment or discrimination.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate of others.
- Communicate constructively and professionally.
- Accept constructive criticism and provide feedback in good faith.
- Respect different opinions, approaches, and levels of experience.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid unnecessary hostility.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or deliberately intimidating other participants.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or persistent bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or collaborative environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, and pull requests.
- Discussions and other communication channels related to Zeen.
- Any public or private interaction where someone represents the Zeen project or its community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

1. A warning and a request to stop the unacceptable behavior.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation in project discussions.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers reserve the right to determine the appropriate response to each situation.
